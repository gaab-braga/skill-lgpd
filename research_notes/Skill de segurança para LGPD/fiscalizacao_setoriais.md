## 1. ANPD sanctions and enforcement actions to date (2023 – Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
ANPD enforcement has moved through three phases:
- **2023–2025:** small fines and mostly warnings or publicization, aimed at one micro-company and many public bodies.
- **2024–2025:** high-profile *preventive measures* (medidas preventivas) with daily fines against Big Tech (Meta AI training, Tools for Humanity/World iris scanning).
- **2026:** real deterrence. ANPD became a regulatory agency (Lei 15.352/2026), opened a record batch of sanctioning proceedings in June 2026, and imposed its first nine-figure fine: **R$ 153.7 million on ByteDance/TikTok, Aug 2026, children's data**.

### Cited Findings

**Telekall Infoservice (first LGPD sanction, July 2023)**
- Small telemarketing company in Espírito Santo. The sanction was published in the DOU on 6 Jul 2023, R$ 14.4 thousand in total — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/06/07/2023/anpd-publica-primeira-sancao-ao-setor-privado-por-descumprimento-da-lgpd/); [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/governo/governo-federal-aplica-a-primeira-multa-por-violacao-da-lgpd-255670/)
- Breakdown: most outlets report **one warning plus two fines of R$ 7,200 each**. Sebrae reports a warning plus one R$ 14.4k fine. The two-fine version is better supported — [Felsberg](https://www.felsberg.com.br/anpd-aplica-primeira-sancao-por-violacao-a-lgpd/); contrast [Sebrae PR](https://sebraepr.com.br/comunidade/artigo/brasil-ja-emitiu-a-primeira-multa-por-descumprimento-a-lgpd)
- Conduct: offered WhatsApp contact lists for electoral campaigns, i.e. it processed publicly available personal data for commercial purposes **without a legal basis**. It also **had no encarregado (DPO)** and failed to answer ANPD requests (warning for non-cooperation) — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/2023/07/07/anpd-estreia-multa-por-violacao-da-lgpd-e-pune-empresa-de-telemarketing/); [Grupo GEN blog](https://blog.grupogen.com.br/juridico/postagens/artigos/a-primeira-sancao-aplicada-pela-anpd/)
- A 25% reduction was available if the company waived appeal (to about R$ 10.8k). This was a first-instance decision, and the outcome of any appeal was not found — [Okai](https://okai.com.br/artigo/anpd-primeira-aplicacao-de-sancao-contra-uma-empresa-por-violacao-a-lei-geral-de-protecao-de-dados-lgpd)

**Public-sector sanctions (2023–2025): warnings and publicization, no fines**
- Public bodies in health, social security, education and social assistance mainly received warnings, publicization, and orders to notify data subjects — [Turivius](https://turivius.com/portal/multas-da-lgpd/)
- **INSS (2024):** sanctioned for **failing to communicate a security incident** that exposed registration, health and financial data of millions of beneficiaries. ANPD ordered **publicization of the infraction on the INSS website and the "Meu INSS" app for 60 days** — [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-sanciona-inss-e-secretaria-de-educacao-do-df-por-violacoes-a-lgpd); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/401393/anpd-condena-inss-por-vazamento-de-dados)
- **Ministério da Saúde (2024):** two warnings. One was under art. 23, III LGPD for having no designated encarregado at the time. The other was a warning plus corrective measure for **not producing a Relatório de Impacto (RIPD) when ANPD requested it**. ANPD denied the Ministry's appeal — [Tozzini Freire, "Dois anos de fiscalização ANPD"](https://tozzinifreire.com.br/site/conteudo/uploads/dois-anos-de-fiscalizacao-andp-(1)-686d74e14452e.pdf); [ANPD CD 37/2025 votos](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/deliberacoes-do-conselho-diretor-1/circuitos-deliberativos-ano-2025/cd-37-2025-votos.pdf)
- **Secretaria de Estado de Educação do Distrito Federal (SEEDF):** four warnings, one per infraction and no fine. The infractions included no record of processing operations (ROPA, art. 37) and no RIPD after ANPD's request — [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-sanciona-inss-e-secretaria-de-educacao-do-df-por-violacoes-a-lgpd)
- **Correction to the brief:** the education secretariat sanctioned was the **DF's (SEEDF), not São Paulo's**. No sanction of São Paulo's Secretaria de Educação was found. The São Paulo-related entity on the sanction list is **IAMSPE** (Instituto de Assistência Médica ao Servidor Público Estadual de SP), reported for Oct 2023 — [search summary citing Confidata](https://confidata.com.br/blog/mapa-sancoes-anpd-todos-casos-2026). This is secondary and unverified.
- Analytical compilations of all ANPD decisions to early 2025 exist and are good primary-adjacent sources: [Tozzini Freire, Relatório analítico de decisões da ANPD](https://tozzinifreire.com.br/site/conteudo/uploads/relatorio-analitico-de-decisoes-da-anpd-67c05739bd198.pdf) (EN version: [Report on ANPD decisions](https://tozzinifreire.com.br/site/conteudo/uploads/reportonanpddecisions-67c1ab2520a23.pdf)); [Mayer Brown, "ANPD applies first sanctions of 2024"](https://www.mayerbrown.com/pt/insights/publications/2024/02/anpd-applies-first-sanctions-of-2024)
- One summary states that **until early 2026 only one monetary fine had been applied to the private sector** (Telekall) — [dponet / search summary](https://dponet.com.br/blog/anpd-2026-fiscalizacao-lgpd-empresas-sancoes/); [Turivius](https://turivius.com/portal/multas-da-lgpd/)

**Meta – AI training (2024)**
- On 2 Jul 2024 (DOU; some sources say decided 1 Jul) ANPD issued a **preventive measure** suspending the provisions of Meta's new privacy policy that allowed personal data to be used to train generative AI. The deadline was 5 days, with a **daily fine of R$ 50,000** for non-compliance. ANPD found **legitimate interest "inadequate"** as the legal basis in that context. The complaint came from Idec — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/02/07/2024/anpd-determina-suspensao-da-nova-politica-de-privacidade-da-meta/); [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/governo/anpd-suspende-uso-de-dados-do-facebook-e-instagram-para-treinar-ia/); [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-determina-suspensao-cautelar-do-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-para-treinamento-da-ia-da-meta)
- ANPD rejected Meta's reconsideration request and kept the measure in place — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/mercado/anpd-rejeita-recurso-da-meta-e-barra-treinamento-de-ia-com-dados-pessoais-295231/)
- On **30 Aug 2024 (Despacho Decisório nº 33/2024/PR/ANPD)** ANPD approved Meta's **compliance plan** and lifted the measure on four conditions:
  - Updated notices and banners about AI training.
  - An easy **right to object (opt-out)**.
  - At least **30 days** between notifying users and starting to use their data.
  - **No training on data from users under 18** until ANPD's final decision.
  
  The CGF monitors implementation. Idec criticized the analysis as insufficient — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/30/08/2024/anpd-aprova-plano-de-conformidade-da-meta-e-derruba-medida-preventiva/); [Okai – Despacho 33/2024](https://okai.com.br/documento/2024-08-30/despacho-decisorio-nº-33-2024-pr-anpd); [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/governo/anpd-libera-uso-de-dados-para-meta-treinar-ia); [Teletime – Idec](https://teletime.com.br/30/08/2024/para-idec-decisao-da-anpd-sobre-ia-da-meta-teve-analise-insuficiente/)

**X Corp (Twitter) – AI training (2024) and Grok (2026)**
- In July 2024 ANPD opened a **fiscalização (inspection) process** against X Corp. over the use of user data to train generative AI (Grok) and sent a formal request (ofício). When X changed its terms in Oct 2024 to make data collection for AI mandatory with no opt-out, ANPD summoned the company and warned that non-response could be treated as **obstruction of inspection (a serious infraction)** — [Aos Fatos](https://www.aosfatos.org/noticias/anpd-x-uso-dados-treinar-ia/); [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/governo/anpd-investiga-x-por-treinamento-de-ia-e-cobra-explicacoes/); [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2024/10/21/seguranca/anpd-x-ex-twitter-tera-que-se-explicar-por-nova-politica-de-privacidade-polemica-de-ia/)
- **Nota Técnica nº 1/2026/FIS/CGF/ANPD**, process **00261.000178/2026-27**, against X Corp. Only a fragment was visible. It refers to an **omission in proactively removing abusive images** in connection with AI on the X platform (the Grok image-generation controversy) — [ANPD NT 1/2026 PDF](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/documentos-tecnicos-orientativos/nota-tecnica-no-1-2026-fis-cgf-anpd.pdf/@@download/file); [Congresso em Foco – caso Grok](https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/artigo/116798/caso-grok-coloca-a-regulacao-a-prova-no-brasil). **Unverified:** conclusions and any sanction.

**Tools for Humanity (World/Worldcoin) – iris biometrics (2025)**
- **Jan 2025:** preventive measure suspending the offer of crypto (WLD) or any financial compensation in exchange for iris collection. The reasoning was that payment taints free consent for sensitive biometric data — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/12/02/2025/anpd-nega-recurso-da-empresa-que-realizava-coleta-de-iris-em-troca-de-criptomoedas/); [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/25/03/2025/anpd-coleta-de-iris-tese/)
- **Feb 2025: Despacho Decisório PR/ANPD nº 6/2025** denied the appeal. World announced it was temporarily stopping iris collection in Brazil — [Okai – Despacho 6/2025](https://okai.com.br/documento/2025-02-11/despacho-decisorio-pr-anpd-nº-6-2025); [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2025/02/11/noticias/empresa-de-sam-altman-suspende-coleta-de-iris-no-brasil/)
- **25 Mar 2025: Despacho Decisório PR/ANPD nº 18/2025** denied a further appeal and set a **daily fine of R$ 50,000** if paid collection resumed — [Okai – Despacho 18/2025](https://okai.com.br/documento/2025-03-25/despacho-decisorio-pr-anpd-nº-18-2025); [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/26/03/2025/anpd-nega-novo-recurso-apresentado-pela-tfh-por-desconformidade-com-a-lgpd/); [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/governo-mantem-proibicao-de-pagamento-por-venda-de-iris)
- **18 Jul 2025:** ANPD rejected TFH's proposed **referral programme**, because the benefit was still tied to collecting third parties' biometric data. TFH said it disagreed and would consider legal measures. Idec supported ANPD — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2025/08/06/reviews/world-brasil-mantem-proibicao-de-pagamento-por-escaneamento-de-olhos/); [Idec](https://idec.org.br/release/idec-apoia-decisao-de-relator-do-caso-em-que-anpd-propoe-manter-suspensao-de-pagamentos-por); Conselho Diretor votes: [CD 17/2025 votos](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/deliberacoes-do-conselho-diretor-1/circuitos-deliberativos-ano-2025/cd-17-2025-votos.pdf/@@download/file)

**ByteDance Brasil / TikTok – children and adolescents (Aug 2026): largest ANPD fine to date**
- On **25 Aug 2026 (DOU)** ANPD fined **ByteDance Brasil Tecnologia Ltda. R$ 153.7 million**. The infractions:
  - Processing children's and adolescents' data **without a valid legal basis**, by letting people use the feed in the **unregistered ("logged-out") experience**.
  - **Failing to adopt effective measures to stop under-13s from registering**.
  
  ByteDance must also **delete all irregularly collected data** and follow a **compliance plan**. It can appeal to the Conselho Diretor within 10 business days. Waiving the appeal and paying earns a 25% reduction, to R$ 115.3 million — [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202608/anpd-multa-tiktok-em-r-153-7-milhoes-por-falhas-na-protecao-de-dados-de-criancas-e-adolescentes); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/463088/anpd-multa-tiktok-em-r-153-7-milhoes-por-falha-na-protecao-de-menores); [Data Privacy Brasil](https://www.dataprivacybr.org/tiktok-e-a-protecao-de-criancas-e-adolescentes-o-que-esta-por-tras-da-multa-de-r-1537-milhoes-da-anpd/); [Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2026/08/25/anpd-multa-tiktok-em-r-1537-milhoes-por-falhas-na-protecao-de-criancas-e-adolescentes/)
- Note: R$ 153.7M exceeds the R$ 50M per-infraction cap (art. 52, II LGPD), so it **must be a sum over multiple infractions**. The per-infraction breakdown was not found in the snippets.

**2026 institutional change and the June 2026 sanctioning wave**
- **Lei 15.352, signed 25 Feb 2026**, converted MP 1.317/2025 (Sept 2025). It turned ANPD into the **Agência Nacional de Proteção de Dados**, a special-regime autarchy linked to the Ministry of Justice, now listed under the Lei das Agências Reguladoras (Lei 13.848/2019). It also created a career with about 200 posts. The same law fixed the ECA Digital's entry into force — [Congresso em Foco](https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/116782/lula-sanciona-lei-que-transforma-anpd-em-agencia-reguladora); [Conjur](https://www.conjur.com.br/2026-mai-10/anpd-afinal-chegou-o-fim-da-indefinicao//?print=1); [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/26/02/2026/lei-que-torna-anpd-agencia-e-fixa-vigencia-do-eca-digital-e-sancionada/)

  - "**19 new PAS**" — [search summary, dponet/Confidata](https://dponet.com.br/blog/anpd-2026-fiscalizacao-lgpd-empresas-sancoes/)

**Legal framework for sanctions (reported)**
- Simple fine up to **2% of the Brazilian revenue of the group/conglomerate, capped at R$ 50 million per infraction**. ANPD can also impose daily fines, publicization, blocking or deletion of data, and suspension or prohibition of processing — [Turivius](https://turivius.com/portal/multas-da-lgpd/); [Barbieri Advogados](https://www.barbieriadvogados.com/multa-lgpd/)
- ANPD does not fine automatically for a leak. It weighs the controller's conduct before, during and after the incident: reasonable security measures, timely notice to ANPD and data subjects, and post-incident response — [Barbieri Advogados](https://barbieriadvogados.com/?p=8822)

### Inferences
- In every concluded case before 2026, **procedural and governance failures** drove the sanction: no DPO, no ROPA, no RIPD on request, failure to notify incidents, failure to answer ANPD. The skill should treat "can we answer ANPD with an encarregado, a ROPA, a RIPD and an incident log within days?" as the baseline control.
- For Big Tech, ANPD acts through **fast preventive measures with daily fines** (R$ 50k/day was the standard figure in both Meta and TFH). A product launch that relies on legitimate interest for AI training, or on paid biometric collection, can be frozen within days.
- TikTok (2026) shows that **logged-out or unregistered experiences still process children's data**, and that age-gating by self-declaration is not "effective". This aligns with ECA Digital (section 6).

## 2. ANPD priority themes ("Mapa de Temas Prioritários")

### Takeaway
- **2024–2025 map (Res. CD/ANPD 10/2023):** data-subject rights, children online, AI, and **scraping and data aggregators**.
- **2026–2027 map (Res. CD/ANPD 30/2025):** at least 75 inspection actions, with focus on data-subject rights, sensitive health, financial and biometric data, children, AI, and the public sector.

### Cited Findings
- **Resolução CD/ANPD nº 10, of 5 Dec 2023** approved the MTP 2024–2025 and the biennial monitoring cycle — [Okai](https://okai.com.br/documento/2023-12-13/resolucao-cd-anpd-nº-10-de-5-de-dezembro-de-2023); [SMA Brasil](https://smabr.com/anpd-autoridade-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados/)
- The four themes for 2024–2025 were data-subject rights, **children and adolescents on digital platforms**, **artificial intelligence**, and **data scraping (raspagem) and data aggregators (data brokers)**. The plan for scraping was operational verifications in 2025 and guidance measures in 2H2025 — [Baptista Luz alert](https://www.baptista.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Alerta-Legal_Inteligência-Artificial-é-prioridade-na-agenda-da-ANPD-para-2024-2025.pdf); [ANPD NT 19/2023](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/documentos-e-publicacoes/documentos-de-publicacoes/nota-tecnica-no-19-2023-fis-cgf-anpd.pdf/@@download/file)
- Telecom was explicitly in the 2024–2025 map — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/22/12/2023/telecom-esta-no-mapa-de-prioridades-da-anpd-para-proximo-bienio/)
- ANPD published a **Relatório de Ciclo de Monitoramento 2023–2025** and a **Balanço de atividades de fiscalização 2024–2025** — [ANPD Relatório](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/RelatorioCiclodeMonitoramento20232025.pdf); [ANPD Balanço](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/acoes-e-programas/governanca/governanca-estrategica/balanco-de-atividades-de-fiscalizacao-2024-2025.pdf/@@display-file/file)
- **Resolução CD/ANPD nº 30, of 23 Dec 2025** approved the **MTP 2026–2027**, in force on publication. It is organised into four axes. **Axis 1** covers data-subject rights, with focus on **sensitive health, financial/credit and biometric data**. The map has at least **75 inspection actions**, including **25 on data-subject rights** and **10 on biometric, health or financial data processing**. It also has a dedicated **AI chapter**, plus children and adolescents online and public-sector actions. A press figure of about 20 public-sector actions starting in 2027 is unverified — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/23/01/2026/anpd-define-75-fiscalizacoes-para-bienio-2026-2027-confira-prioridades/); [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/intellectual-property/brazil-anpd-publishes-map-of-priority-issues-for-oversight-and-regulatory-action-2026-2027-biennium-and-update-of-the-regulatory-agenda-for-the-2025-2026-biennium); [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-publica-mapa-de-temas-prioritarios-para-o-bienio-2026-2027-e-atualiza-agenda-regulatoria-2025-2026); [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/governo/anpd-mais-funcionarios-mais-aperto-na-fiscalizacao-biometria-ia-e-poder-publico-sao-prioridades/); [ANPD NT 54/2025](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/nota-tecnica-no-54-2025-fis-cgf-anpd.pdf)
- In parallel, **Resolução CD/ANPD nº 31/2025** updated the **Agenda Regulatória 2025–2026** (16 initiatives in four priority phases). The next map (2028–2029) is due by 30 Nov 2027 — [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-publica-mapa-de-temas-prioritarios-para-o-bienio-2026-2027-e-atualiza-agenda-regulatoria-2025-2026); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/447187/prioridades-regulatorias-da-anpd-diante-da-resolucao-cd-anpd-30)

### Inferences
- For a developer-facing skill, the highest-risk product categories through 2027 are:
  - Health apps and health-tech.
  - Fintech, credit scoring and Open Finance.
  - Biometric authentication and facial recognition.
  - Children-accessible products.
  - Generative AI features.
  - Public-sector systems.
  - Scraping or enrichment pipelines.
- Self-service data-subject rights (DSAR) flows are a recurring inspection target (25 actions in the 2026–27 map).

## 3. Common violation patterns observed in ANPD practice

### Takeaway
The recurring failures are:
- Not communicating security incidents (most frequent).
- No designated encarregado.
- No ROPA or RIPD when ANPD asks.
- No legal basis, especially reuse of "public" data, AI training on legitimate interest, and children's data.
- Consent tainted by payment (biometrics).
- Non-cooperation with ANPD.

### Cited Findings
- The most frequent infraction in past ANPD cases is **failure to notify ANPD and data subjects about a security incident** — [Turivius](https://turivius.com/portal/multas-da-lgpd/); [Confidata map (snippet)](https://confidata.com.br/blog/mapa-sancoes-anpd-todos-casos-2026). Example: INSS, publicization for 60 days — [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-sanciona-inss-e-secretaria-de-educacao-do-df-por-violacoes-a-lgpd)
- **Missing encarregado (art. 41 / art. 23, III):** Telekall and Ministério da Saúde. In June 2026, 21 entities were referred for not answering ANPD's DPO information requests — [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/2023/07/07/anpd-estreia-multa-por-violacao-da-lgpd-e-pune-empresa-de-telemarketing/); [Tozzini Freire](https://tozzinifreire.com.br/site/conteudo/uploads/dois-anos-de-fiscalizacao-andp-(1)-686d74e14452e.pdf); [Confidata (snippet)](https://confidata.com.br/blog/lista-empresas-na-mira-da-anpd-2026)
- **No RIPD on request / no ROPA:** Ministério da Saúde and SEEDF — [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-sanciona-inss-e-secretaria-de-educacao-do-df-por-violacoes-a-lgpd)
- **No legal basis:**
  - Commercialising publicly accessible contact data (Telekall) — [Okai](https://okai.com.br/artigo/anpd-primeira-aplicacao-de-sancao-contra-uma-empresa-por-violacao-a-lei-geral-de-protecao-de-dados-lgpd)
  - Legitimate interest held inadequate for training AI on users' posts (Meta) — [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/governo/anpd-suspende-uso-de-dados-do-facebook-e-instagram-para-treinar-ia/)
  - Children's data in a logged-out feed (TikTok) — [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202608/anpd-multa-tiktok-em-r-153-7-milhoes-por-falhas-na-protecao-de-dados-de-criancas-e-adolescentes)
- **Biometrics with financial inducement** vitiating consent (TFH/World) — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/25/03/2025/anpd-coleta-de-iris-tese/)
- **Lack of transparency and no opt-out** for AI training (X, Meta) — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2024/10/21/seguranca/anpd-x-ex-twitter-tera-que-se-explicar-por-nova-politica-de-privacidade-polemica-de-ia/); [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/30/08/2024/anpd-aprova-plano-de-conformidade-da-meta-e-derruba-medida-preventiva/)
- **Scraping and data aggregators** are a declared ANPD priority theme (MTP 2024–2025) — [Baptista Luz](https://www.baptista.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Alerta-Legal_Inteligência-Artificial-é-prioridade-na-agenda-da-ANPD-para-2024-2025.pdf)
- **Non-cooperation / obstruction** of inspection is treated as aggravating or as a separate infraction (Telekall warning; X warned) — [Grupo GEN](https://blog.grupogen.com.br/juridico/postagens/artigos/a-primeira-sancao-aplicada-pela-anpd/); [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2024/10/21/seguranca/anpd-x-ex-twitter-tera-que-se-explicar-por-nova-politica-de-privacidade-polemica-de-ia/)
- **Incident communication rule (Resolução CD/ANPD nº 15/2024):**
  - Notify ANPD and data subjects within **3 business days** of learning that personal data were affected, when there is relevant risk or harm.
  - Supplementary information may follow within **20 business days**.
  - Keep a **register of all incidents (including those not notified) for at least 5 years**.
  - Small processing agents (Res. 2/2022) get **double deadlines**.
  - The earlier guidance was 2 business days.
  
  — [Cescon Barrieu](https://cesconbarrieu.com.br/anpd-aprova-o-regulamento-de-comunicacao-de-incidente-de-seguranca/); [Machado Meyer](https://www.machadomeyer.com.br/pt/inteligencia-juridica/publicacoes-ij/direito-digital/anpd-publica-regulamento-de-comunicacao-de-incidente-de-seguranca); [Legismap](https://legismap.com.br/conteudos/artigos-e-noticias/anpd-publica-resolucao-que-aprova-o-regulamento-de-comunicacao-de-incidente-de-seguranca)

### Inferences
- Developer-facing controls that map directly to ANPD's sanction history:
  - An incident-detection-to-notification runbook built for the 3-business-day clock.
  - An immutable incident register kept 5+ years.
  - A ROPA and DPIA (RIPD) generated from code and data inventories.
  - A published encarregado contact.
  - A documented legal basis per purpose, with an LIA (legitimate interest assessment) for any legitimate-interest processing, especially AI training.
  - Age assurance for any product likely to be accessed by children.
  - No incentives tied to sensitive-data collection.
  - Scraping and enrichment reviewed for legal basis and transparency.

### Gaps
- No quantitative ANPD statistics on infraction frequency were read directly. The "most frequent" claim comes from secondary blogs.

## 4. Notable Brazilian data leaks and lessons

### Takeaway
- The 2021 "megavazamento" (about 223M CPFs) was never officially attributed to a source. Serasa denied it was the origin, and litigation and consumer-agency notices followed.
- The Banco Central publicly announces **Pix-key data incidents** almost every year. These involve registration data only, and the instruction to customers is that notices come only through the institution's app or internet banking.

### Cited Findings
- **Megavazamento (Jan 2021):** data of about **223 million people** (CPFs, including deceased persons), plus 40 million companies and 104 million vehicles. Some packages had up to 37 data categories — [Tecnoblog, one year on](https://tecnoblog.net/especiais/megavazamento-de-223-milhoes-de-cpfs-um-ano-se-passou-e-ainda-ha-perguntas-sem-resposta/); [Tecnoblog, "organizado demais"](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/2022/01/31/megavazamento-de-cpfs-tinha-algo-que-nao-e-normal-ser-organizado-demais/)
- **19 Mar 2021, PF "Operação Deepwater":** arrest in Uberlândia (MG) of a hacker suspected of selling or spreading the data. ANPD praised the operation. ANPD and PF (under STF order) investigated — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/19/03/2021/anpd-comemora-operacao-da-pf-que-prendeu-hacker-suspeito-por-megavazamentos/); [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2021/03/19/seguranca/hacker-afirma-que-megavazamento-de-janeiro-nao-comecou-no-serasa/)
- **Serasa** denied being the source and reported no evidence of a breach on its side. **Senacon and Procon-SP notified Serasa**, and **Instituto Sigilo sued for at least R$ 200 million in collective moral damages** — [Tecnoblog – Senacon/Procon](https://tecnoblog.net/405877/senacon-e-procon-sp-notificam-serasa-sobre-vazamento-de-220-milhoes-de-cpfs/); [Tecnoblog – ação R$ 200 mi](https://tecnoblog.net/412923/acao-judicial-pede-multa-de-r-200-milhoes-a-serasa-por-vazamento/)

  - Jan 2022: about 160k keys (Banese)
  - 2023: several incidents
  - 2024: Qesh, about 533k keys; 99Pay (Jul 2024)
  - Mar 2026: **Pefisa (Pernambucanas' finance arm), about 28k keys**, with Agibank and MP-GO bringing the total to about 33.5k
  - 12 May 2026: Credifit SCD
  - 15 Jun 2026: 828 keys, institution unverified
  
  BCB states that the data are registration data only, with no passwords, balances or transactions exposed, and that affected users are informed **only via their institution's app or internet banking** — [Correio Braziliense 2022](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/economia/2022/01/amp/4979274-banco-central-comunica-vazamento-de-dados-de-160-mil-chaves-pix.html); [Correio Braziliense 2024 – Qesh](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/economia/2024/09/amp/6954011-bc-comunica-vazamento-de-dados-de-533-mil-chaves-pix-da-qesh.html); [O Povo – 99Pay](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/economia/2024/07/10/bc-comunica-vazamento-de-dados-pessoais-vinculados-a-chaves-do-pix-pela-99pay.html); [Olhar Digital – Pefisa](https://olhardigital.com.br/2026/03/21/seguranca/banco-central-comunica-vazamento-de-mais-de-28-mil-chaves-pix-da-pefisa/); [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/pagamentos/bc-comunica-terceiro-vazamento-de-chaves-pix-no-ano-incidente-atingiu-financeira-da-pernambucanas/); [TNH1](https://www.tnh1.com.br/variedades/banco-central-emite-comunicado-gravissimo-para-usuarios-do-pix-apos-vazamento-de-dados/)
- A report mentions **11 million Pix keys** communicated jointly by BCB and CNJ (date not identified in the snippet) — [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/bc-e-cnj-comunicam-vazamento-de-11-milhoes-de-chaves-pix/)
- **Enel:** the STJ's leading "no presumed moral damage" case (AREsp 2.130.619) involved a **leak at an electricity distributor** (Enel/Eletropaulo, São Paulo) — [Dizer o Direito](https://www.dizerodireito.com.br/2023/03/o-vazamento-de-dados-pessoais-nao-gera.html)

### Inferences
- Lessons for the skill:
  - Brazil's ecosystem of leaked CPF-linked datasets drives fraud (phishing, "golpe do Pix", SIM swap). Treat CPF plus phone plus address as high-risk even though it is not "sensitive" in law.
  - Minimise collection of CPF, keep it out of logs, and mask it in UIs.
  - Third-party and vendor access is the usual root cause of Pix incidents ("falhas pontuais em sistemas" of a participant).
  - Customer communications must avoid links (anti-phishing), following BCB's model.

- The **C&M Software attack of July 2025** (Pix/STR connectivity provider, reportedly hundreds of millions of reais diverted) is widely reported, but no source in these searches linked it to CMN 5.274/2025. It is unverified here.

## 5. Judicial interpretation (STF and STJ)

### Takeaway
- The STF recognised data protection as an **autonomous fundamental right** in ADI 6387 (2020, IBGE/MP 954), and **EC 115/2022** constitutionalised it (art. 5, LXXIX).
- The STJ's 2nd Panel (AREsp 2.130.619, Mar 2023) held that leaks of **ordinary** data do not generate presumed moral damage. Since 2025 the 3rd Panel has presumed damage for **sensitive data** (life insurance, REsp 2.121.904) and even for credit-score data (REsp 2.201.694). Case law is in flux.

### Cited Findings
- **ADI 6387 (and ADIs 6388, 6389, 6390, 6393):** filed by the OAB against **MP 954/2020** (17 Apr 2020), which required telecom operators to share the names, phone numbers and addresses of more than 230M consumers with IBGE during the pandemic. Rapporteur **Min. Rosa Weber** suspended it on **24 Apr 2020**, citing the absence of technical or administrative safeguards against unauthorised access, leaks or misuse. The **Plenary upheld the injunction 10–1 (May 2020)**, which is read as recognising an autonomous fundamental right to data protection — [OAB](https://www.oab.org.br/util/print/58130?print=Noticia); [Conjur](https://www.conjur.com.br/2020-abr-24/rosa-atende-oab-suspende-mp-compartilhamento-dados//?print=1); [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/07/05/2020/stf-mantem-suspensa-mp-de-compartilhamento-de-dados-das-operadoras-com-o-ibge/); [Data Privacy Brasil amicus](https://www.dataprivacybr.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/dpbrr_amicuscuria_stf_ibge.pdf)
- **EC 115/2022** inserted personal data protection into **art. 5, LXXIX** of the Constitution — [Conjur](https://www.conjur.com.br/2022-fev-24/ferreira-inconstitucionalidade-decreto-100462019-stf//?print=1)
- **AREsp 2.130.619/SP (STJ, 2ª Turma, Rel. Min. Francisco Falcão, j. 7 Mar 2023, Informativo 766):** a leak of ordinary personal data from an electricity utility does not by itself generate presumed (in re ipsa) moral damage. The data subject must prove concrete harm. The ruling distinguished sensitive data (art. 11), where the outcome could differ — [Dizer o Direito](https://www.dizerodireito.com.br/2023/03/o-vazamento-de-dados-pessoais-nao-gera.html); [Trilhante – Info 766](https://informativos.trilhante.com.br/informativos/informativo-766-stj/stj-aresp-2130619-sp); [Machado Meyer](https://www.machadomeyer.com.br/pt/imprensa-ij/stj-e-a-ausencia-de-dano-moral-in-re-ipsa-em-vazamentos-de-dados). Doctrinal criticism: [Revista IBERC](https://revistaiberc.emnuvens.com.br/iberc/article/download/258/220/1524)
- **REsp 2.121.904/SP (STJ, 3ª Turma, Rel. Min. Nancy Andrighi, Feb/Mar 2025; sources differ on the month):** in a life-insurance contract, a **leak of sensitive data** provided by the insured gives rise to **strict liability** of the insurer and **presumed moral damage** — [Trilhante](https://informativos.trilhante.com.br/julgados/stj-resp-2121904-sp); [Damásio](https://juris.damasio.com.br/jurisprudencia/julgado/842/responsabilidade-objetiva-e-dano-moral-presumido-por-vazamento-de-dados-sensiveis-no-seguro-de-vida); [Correio Braziliense](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/direito-e-justica/2025/03/amp/7069567-o-preco-da-negligencia-no-vazamento-de-dados-em-seguros-de-vida.html)
- **REsp 2.147.374/SP (STJ, 3ª Turma, Dec 2024):** leak of non-sensitive data. Commentators **disagree** on whether it presumed damage — [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/431249/responsabilidade-civil-por-dados-vazados-resp-2-147-374-sp); contrast [Migalhas, "STJ reconhece dano moral presumido"](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/431132/stj-reconhece-dano-moral-presumido-em-vazamento-de-dados-pessoais). **Unresolved.**
- **REsp 2.201.694/SP (STJ, 3ª Turma, Rel. Min. Nancy Andrighi, Aug 2025, majority):** presumed moral damage where personal data were improperly shared or used for **credit-score formation**, even without sensitive data — [search summary, Rota Jurídica](https://www.rotajuridica.com.br/?p=165960); [Damásio – Info 871 credit-data case](https://juris.damasio.com.br/jurisprudencia/julgado/871/compartilhamento-de-dados-pessoais-no-credito-lgpd-consentimento-e-dano-moral-nao-presumido). **Note:** the Damásio headline for the related Info 871 case says "dano moral não presumido". The sources conflict, and the full judgments must be checked.

### Inferences
- Civil litigation risk depends on data category. Leaks of **sensitive data (health, biometrics, genetic, religion and others), and possibly credit-score data**, now carry presumed-damage exposure in the 3rd Panel. Ordinary-data leaks still generally require proof of harm in the 2nd Panel line.
- The skill should flag sensitive-data fields as carrying both higher ANPD risk and class or individual damages risk.

### Gaps
- Whether ADI 6387's merits were judged or the case was dismissed after MP 954 lapsed.
- Official ementas of REsp 2.147.374, 2.121.904 and 2.201.694.
- Whether the STJ's 2nd Section (which unifies the 3rd and 4th Panels) has settled the issue.
- STF Tema 1.141 / ADPF 695 (data sharing within public administration, Decree 10.046/2019) appears in the Conjur result but was not researched.

## 6. Sector-specific rules that add obligations beyond the LGPD

### Takeaway
Regulated sectors carry overlapping duties: dual incident reporting, cybersecurity policies, cloud and vendor controls, retention mandates, and special consent regimes. The sectors covered:
- **Financial:** CMN 4.893/2021 as amended by CMN 5.274/2025; BCB 85/2021 as amended by BCB 538/2025; Open Finance; Pix.
- **Health:** Lei 13.787/2018; CFM 1.821/2007.
- **Telecom:** Anatel R-Ciber (Res. 740/2020, as amended by Res. 767/2024).
- **Internet:** Marco Civil.
- **Children's online products:** ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025, in force 17 Mar 2026), supervised by ANPD.
- **Cybercrime:** Lei 14.155/2021.

### Cited Findings

**Financial sector – cybersecurity and cloud**
- **Resolução CMN 4.893/2021** (banks and other BCB-authorised institutions) sets the cybersecurity policy and the rules for contracting data-processing, storage and cloud services. **Resolução BCB 85/2021** is the counterpart for payment institutions and similar entities — [Vortex guide](https://vortex.security/guias/resolucao-cmn-4893-bcb-85/); [Cadoc](https://cadoc.ai/post/resolucao-cmn-4893)
- **Resolução CMN 5.274, of 18 Dec 2025 (DOU 22 Dec 2025)** amends 4.893. It reportedly adds **14 mandatory minimum cybersecurity controls**, oriented to verifiable evidence:
  - Penetration testing.
  - Authentication.
  - Access control with periodic permission review.
  - Traceability.
  - Specific rules for Pix and STR connectivity.
  - Stronger **third-party management**.
  
  **BCB Resolution 538/2025** reportedly makes the corresponding change to BCB 85. One source gives the adaptation deadline as **1 Mar 2026** (unverified) — [CMN Voto 88/2025](https://normativos.bcb.gov.br/Votos/CMN/202588/Voto_do_CMN_88_2025.pdf); [LegisWeb](https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=488277); [Tenchi Security](https://www.tenchisecurity.com/br/insights-news/resolucao-cmn-5-274-o-que-muda-na-gestao-de-terceiros-ate-marco-de-2026); [Niuco](https://otimize.niuco.com.br/2026/05/12/compliance-bancario-bacen/)

**Open Finance**
- **Resolução Conjunta nº 1/2020** originally capped consent at **12 months** (except payment initiation). **Resolução Conjunta nº 7/2023 (26 Oct 2023)** removed the cap: the term now only needs to be compatible with the purposes, and renewal was simplified — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/26/10/2023/bc-tira-barreira-de-renovacao-de-opt-in-a-cada-12-meses-no-open-finance/); [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202310/bc-simplifica-renovacao-de-consentimentos-no-open-finance-e-amplia-prazo-de-validade-do-compartilhamento); [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/regulamentacao/bc-acaba-com-limite-de-12-meses-para-compartilhamento-de-dados-no-open-finance); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/340101/o-consentimento-no-open-banking-e-na-lgpd)
- **Oct 2026:** CMN and BCB reformulated the rules on Open Finance partnerships and governance. A reported **Resolução Conjunta nº 21 (22 Oct 2026)** bars the transmitting institution from prompting customers to revoke consent and deems consent void when there is well-founded suspicion of fraud. This comes from a secondary source and is unverified — [Cescon Barrieu](https://cesconbarrieu.com.br/open-finance-cmn-e-bcb-reformulam-as-regras-de-parcerias-e-de-governanca/); [Capital Aberto](https://capitalaberto.com.br/regulacao/2026/10/bc-endurece-regras-de-parcerias-no-open-finance)

**Pix incidents**
- BCB publicly communicates incidents involving Pix-key registration data held by participants. Customers are notified only via the institution's app or internet banking — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2026/03/21/seguranca/banco-central-comunica-vazamento-de-mais-de-28-mil-chaves-pix-da-pefisa/); [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/bc-comunica-vazamento-de-dados-de-chaves-pix-em-fintech/)

**Health**
- **Lei 13.787/2018:** governs digitisation and electronic storage of patient records (prontuário) together with the LGPD (art. 1). Paper and digitised records must be kept for **20 years from the last entry**, after which they may be eliminated. Elimination requires secrecy and a formal record of the act (art. 6) — [Lei 13.787 (Câmara)](https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/lei/2018/lei-13787-27-dezembro-2018-787543-publicacaooriginal-157119-pl.html); [CFM 2026 document](https://portal.cfm.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/19_2026.pdf); [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/367003/a-lgpd-e-o-tempo-de-guarda-dos-prontuarios-medicos)
- **Resolução CFM 1.821/2007**, partly amended or revoked by CFM 2.218/2018, requires **ICP-Brasil digital certificates** (NGS2 security level) for digitisation and electronic records. Sources **conflict** on whether natively electronic records need **permanent** retention or 20 years — [CFM 1.821/2007](https://sistemas.cfm.org.br/normas/arquivos/resolucoes/BR/2007/1821_2007.pdf); [Certisign](https://blog-homolog.certisign.com.br/prontuario-medico-guarda-e-manuseio-de-documentos/amp)
- Health data is in Axis 1 of the MTP 2026–2027, and hospitals and clinics are flagged as inspection targets — [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/23/01/2026/anpd-define-75-fiscalizacoes-para-bienio-2026-2027-confira-prioridades/)
- Lei 15.352/2026 was presented as reinforcing data protection in SUS digital transformation — [Agência Gov](https://agenciagov.ebc.com.br/noticias/202603/protecao-de-dados-pessoais-ganha-reforco-institucional-e-fortalece-transformacao-digital-no-sus)

**Telecom – Anatel R-Ciber**
- **Resolução Anatel 740, of 21 Dec 2020 (R-Ciber):** collective-interest carriers (except small ones) must have a board-approved cybersecurity policy, use suppliers with their own policies and independent audits, carry out vulnerability assessments, file annual reports, and notify relevant incidents to Anatel — [GlobalSuite](https://www.globalsuitesolutions.com/pt/?p=117753)
- **Resolução Anatel 767/2024 (Aug 2024):** carriers **of any size** must notify Anatel of incidents that must also be notified to the ANPD. It extended obligations to small carriers (PPPs), added prior evaluation of suppliers, and signalled future regulation of data centres and cloud — [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/pt/insights/publications/2024/08/amendment-to-anatels-cybersecurity-regulations-incident-notification-and-prior-evaluation-of-suppliers); [Demarest](https://www.demarest.com.br/anatel-altera-o-regulamento-de-seguranca-cibernetica-e-sinaliza-futura-regulacao-direta-de-data-centers-e-cloud-computing/); [Convergência Digital](https://convergenciadigital.com.br/telecom/anatel-aperta-as-regras-e-amplia-numero-de-empresas-com-obrigacoes-de-ciberseguranca/)

**Children's online products – ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025)**
- In force since **17 Mar 2026**. The original one-year vacatio was vetoed and replaced by six months via **MP 1.319/2025**, and Lei 15.352/2026 fixed the date. A minority of sources say 18 Mar. **ANPD is the supervising authority**. The law applies to any digital product or service **"likely to be accessed" by children or adolescents** — [Congresso em Foco](https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/117235/eca-digital-entra-em-vigor-o-que-muda-com-a-nova-lei); [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/2026/03/16/internet-e-redes-sociais/eca-digital-comeca-a-valer-na-terca-feira-17-saiba-o-que-muda-nas-redes/); [Teletime](https://teletime.com.br/26/02/2026/lei-que-torna-anpd-agencia-e-fixa-vigencia-do-eca-digital-e-sancionada/)
- **Self-declaration of age is not enough.** Effective age-verification mechanisms are mandatory. ANPD set **six minimum requirements** for age-verification mechanisms and a **three-phase rollout timetable**, plus a two-phase inspection plan. Sanctions under **art. 35** run from warning to fines to suspension or prohibition of activities — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/internet/pontos-para-entender-o-eca-digital-que-entra-em-vigor-nesta-terca-17/); [Tecnoblog](https://tecnoblog.net/noticias/eca-digital-entra-em-vigor-para-proteger-menores-na-internet-veja-mudancas/); [BMA](https://www.bmalaw.com.br/conteudo/direito-digital-protecao-de-dados-e-cybersecurity/informativos-e-newsletters/eca-digital-e-sua-regulamentacao-entram-em-vigor/pdf); [Computer Weekly BR](https://www.computerweekly.com/br/reportagen/ECA-Digital-obrigacoes-penalidades-e-o-que-muda-para-plataformas-digitais-no-Brasil)

**Cybercrime – Lei 14.155/2021**
- **Invasion of a computer device (CP art. 154-A)** raised from detention of 3 months–1 year to **imprisonment of 1–4 years plus fine**. The penalty rises by 1/3 to 2/3 if economic loss results. Obtaining private communications or trade secrets carries **2–5 years**.
- **Electronic fraud (art. 171 §2º-A)** and **theft by electronic fraud (art. 155 §4º-B)**: **4–8 years plus fine**.

  — [Planalto – Lei 14.155/2021](https://planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2019-2022/2021/lei/l14155.htm); [Estratégia Concursos](https://www.estrategiaconcursos.com.br/blog/lei-14-155-2021-a-fraude-eletronica-e-outras-alteracoes-no-codigo-penal/); [Projuris](https://www.projuris.com.br/blog/lei-carolina-dieckman-tudo-o-que-voce-precisa-saber-sobre/)

**Marco Civil da Internet (Lei 12.965/2014)**
- Art. 15 requires application providers to keep **application-access logs**. These logs are key evidence in cybercrime cases — [Jus.com.br](https://jus.com.br/artigos/92210/invasao-de-dispositivo-informatico-furto-eletronico-estelionato-eletronico-e-competencia-lei-14-155-21/2). The exact retention periods were not confirmed in the retrieved sources; see Gaps.

### Inferences
- Dual and triple reporting is the norm in regulated sectors:
  - A telecom data breach goes to ANPD (3 business days) and Anatel.
  - A bank or payment-institution breach goes to ANPD and BCB (under 4.893/85), plus Pix-specific communication when DICT data are involved.
  
  The skill should prompt "which sector regulator also needs notice?"
- Retention conflicts are real. Marco Civil, Lei 13.787 and CFM rules, and tax and labour laws impose **minimum** retention periods, while the LGPD demands deletion when the purpose ends. "Legal or regulatory obligation" (art. 7, II / art. 16, I) is the bridge, and every retention period should cite its legal source.
- ECA Digital plus the TikTok fine make age assurance a hard requirement for consumer apps with plausible child audiences, including logged-out experiences.
